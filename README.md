- Permission-controlled file, shell, browser, and workspace operations
- Memory, knowledge-graph, rollback, approval, and audit concepts
- Focus on dependable and inspectable AI automation

### **ForgAI — Deterministic PCB Validation Engine**
*Engineering validation platform for embedded hardware and PCB workflows*

- Checks board identity, duplicate pins, interfaces, I²C configuration, voltage constraints, and data provenance
- Designed to combine AI assistance with deterministic engineering rules
- ESP32/STM32-oriented embedded-system workflows

### **SKYWARDEN-T — ISR / Autonomous Drone Research Platform**
*Experimental platform for telemetry, edge vision, flight-control research, and autonomous-system development*

- Computer-vision and autonomous decision-support research

### **SLIME — Evidence-Driven Autonomous Security Research System**
*Agentic security research architecture focused on controlled experimentation and verification*
- Persistent target and experience memory

- Policy-controlled experiments and replayable verification

- Learns from successful and failed research paths while preserving evidence
